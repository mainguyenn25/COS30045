About Us

Learn more about the purpose of the Power Appliances website.

Our Purpose

Power Appliances is an educational website focused on appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

The purpose of this website is to provide simple and understandable information about how household appliances use electricity and how usage patterns can influence electricity costs.

Our Goals

Educate

Provide accessible information about household appliance energy consumption.

Inform

Help users understand the relationship between appliance usage and electricity costs.

Encourage

Encourage users to consider energy efficiency when using and purchasing appliances.

Website Features

This website includes information about televisions, appliance energy consumption and an interactive energy calculator.

The energy calculator uses JavaScript to calculate estimated electricity consumption and costs based on user input.
