Energy Consumption in Australian Homes

Household appliances play an important role in Australia's electricity consumption. Appliances such as televisions, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners can contribute significantly to household energy usage.

Understanding how much electricity an appliance uses can help households make informed decisions about energy efficiency and reduce unnecessary electricity costs.
